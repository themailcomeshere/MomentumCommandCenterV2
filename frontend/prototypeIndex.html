How to use the handoff
  • Before considering an entry: check 5M permission first. If it is NO, UNKNOWN, or stale, do not treat a 1M move as an authorized setup.
  • When 5M permission is YES: watch 1M for a separate execution confirmation. A high 5M score alone is not an entry signal.
  • How often: review when a new completed 5M bar closes, and inspect 1M as each completed minute arrives. The dashboard refreshes every 15 seconds; it is not a tick feed.
  • After entry: reassess 1M management on each completed minute and recheck 5M structure on each completed 5M bar. Missing or stale data means WAIT / UNKNOWN.
  • Replay: use it after historical bars have been stored to inspect a past decision. It does not predict a future setup or recover a historical broker position.

        MCC WORKFLOW

        Quick Start

        1. Review Morning Command Brief. Check the timestamp, source status, market context, and unavailable inputs.
        2. Refresh Alpaca market movers. Load persisted Alpaca SIP-based discovery events. This refreshes the discovery list; it is not a full-market scan or a qualification decision.
        3. Review Red Zone. When the live discovery group is selected, refreshed candidates populate the Red Zone preview (up to four). Pin a symbol manually to keep it visible, or dismiss a row.
        4. Use Watch deliberately. The Watch action adds a ticker to the separate browser-local Watchlist. It does not pin the ticker to Red Zone and does not automatically qualify or authorize it.
          5. \n
        5. Inspect the Main Chart. Type a symbol in the fixed left-side SYMBOL field or click a Red Zone tile. The chart and decision panels follow the focused symbol.
        6. Use Scan & Analyze. Enter symbols and an Arizona-time window to retrieve 1M/5M bars and inspect source coverage and reasons.
        7. Review the 5M → 1M decision context. Keep higher-timeframe permission separate from the 1M execution event. Missing evidence is not a pass.
        8. Use Boring Overnight separately. Run historical bar and coverage checks; do not treat incomplete history or missing catalyst data as READY.
        9. Provider comparison is diagnostic. Run it only when testing Alpaca vs Massive for a chosen date and timeframe, not every morning.

        This prototype does not connect to Thinkorswim, submit orders, or establish a validated live opportunity ranking. Confirm data source, timestamps, freshness, and actual chart states before acting.
