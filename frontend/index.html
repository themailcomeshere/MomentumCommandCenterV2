        MCC WORKFLOW

        Quick Start

        1. Review Morning Command Brief. Check the timestamp, source status, market context, and unavailable inputs.
        2. Refresh Alpaca market movers. Load persisted discovery events; treat them as candidates, not qualified trades.
        3. Review Red Zone. Inspect up to four candidates. Pin symbols you want to keep visible, dismiss rows you do not want, or restore dismissed rows.
        4. Inspect the Main Chart. Select a symbol or click a Red Zone tile. The chart and decision panels follow the focused symbol.
        5. Use Scan & Analyze. Enter symbols and an Arizona-time window to retrieve 1M/5M bars and inspect source coverage and reasons.
        6. Review the 5M → 1M decision context. Keep higher-timeframe permission separate from the 1M execution event. Missing evidence is not a pass.
        7. Use Boring Overnight separately. Run historical bar and coverage checks; do not treat incomplete history or missing catalyst data as READY.
        8. Provider comparison is diagnostic. Run it only when testing Alpaca vs Massive for a chosen date and timeframe, not every morning.

        This prototype does not connect to Thinkorswim, submit orders, or establish a validated live opportunity ranking. Confirm data source, timestamps, freshness, and actual chart states before acting.
