## **3. The most important current findings**

### **A. Boring Overnight — the largest known data-quality blocker**

Your November 13 CRBG capture is particularly useful because it demonstrates both successful safeguards and unresolved data gaps.

- The historical evaluator requires 20 completed overnight pairs.
- Only **13 of 20** pairs were valid in the capture.
- **Seven historical next-session opening bars** were missing at the exact required 09:30 timestamp.
- The evaluator correctly withheld rolling statistics rather than substituting a nearby minute.
- The November 13 current opening bar was also missing after its expected availability time.
- The 09:30 one-minute bar's Open is only a proxy; it is not verified as the official opening-auction print.

The boundary-audit correction is now visible in the running application: it distinguishes a future endpoint that is not yet available from an endpoint that should exist but is missing.

**What remains:** determine why those seven exact opening bars are absent, whether the provider can reliably supply them, and whether the store can preserve them. Do not weaken the exact-boundary requirement just to make the evaluator READY.

There is a second unresolved question: the capture fetched **6,976 bars but persisted zero new observations**. That may simply mean all observations were already present, but the evidence does not establish that. We need to inspect the persistence and deduplication accounting.

### **B. Technical assessment — safeguards improved, candidate still fails**

The staleness defect was corrected: stale intraday bars can no longer supply technical numeric indicators or V2 decision fields.

In the November 13 CRBG capture, the later technical assessment had:

- Matched Alpaca/IEX provenance.
- Fresh 1-minute and 5-minute bars.
- `intradayBarsUsable=true`.
- `v2DecisionUsable=true`.
- 5-minute permission false.
- 1-minute state `NoTrade/NONE`.
- Extension ATR approximately 2.09.

That means the data passed the technical usability checks, but the setup still did not qualify. This distinction is correct: *usable inputs do not mean a tradable setup.*

Daily qualification values also remained unavailable because IEX coverage is partial. A complete and fresh 20-session Daily history is not sufficient to make that history qualification-usable when the feed does not meet the required coverage.

### **C. Catalyst — correctly conservative, but still needs validation**

The November 12 CRBG evidence contained a Wells Fargo analyst-action headline classified as `MATERIAL_EVENT_REVIEW`.

The later November 13 capture had zero articles and therefore returned `UNKNOWN`, not `CLEAR`.

That is the correct fail-closed behavior. Zero articles cannot establish that there is no catalyst risk. The remaining work is to validate news coverage completeness, source failures, time windows, and classification across a wider candidate set.

### **D. Opportunity Quality — not yet a calibrated ranking system**

The current Opportunity Quality work is a diagnostic classification layer. It is not a calibrated probability of success.

Outstanding work includes:

- Full scanner-pool fixture coverage.
- Mapping every required source field.
- Per-symbol timestamp and freshness verification.
- Calibration against a sufficiently large outcome dataset.
- Explicit acceptance criteria for classifications.

Until those are complete, the score/classification should not be treated as a statistically validated probability or standalone trading signal.
