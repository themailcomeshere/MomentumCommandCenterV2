# SLS +175% on 2026-11-17: could it have been predicted?

Question from the operator, 2026-11-17. Research note; nothing here is a trade instruction.

## What happened

- **Catalyst:** SELLAS Life Sciences reported positive topline results from the pivotal Phase 3 REGAL trial of galinpepimut-S in acute myeloid leukemia.
  - Overall survival HR 0.59, p = 0.035, median 24.0 vs 14.7 months.
  - Sources: [Yahoo Finance (company release)](https://ca.finance.yahoo.com/news/sellas-life-sciences-announces-positive-120000814.html), [StockTitan](https://www.stocktitan.net/news/SLS/sellas-life-sciences-announces-positive-pivotal-phase-3-regal-trial-6uzi9aptgva4.html), [Benzinga](https://www.benzinga.com/trading-ideas/movers/26/11/63344384/sellas-stock-triples-on-cancer-vaccine-cutting-death-risk-by-41-in-pivotal-leukemia-trial).
- **Release time:** Data not found in sources. The Yahoo URL suffix suggests 12:00 UTC (07:00 ET, 05:00 AZ); not confirmed.
- **MCC's news feed:** carried only an 8-K aggregator repost, stamped 2026-11-17 14:01:47 UTC (07:01 AZ). IEX had already printed $29.83 at 06:30 AZ, so MCC's feed lagged the move.

## Was the timing knowable?

The window, yes; the day and the direction, no.

- **The trigger:** the final analysis was triggered by the 80th death.
  - SELLAS announced reaching it on about 2026-10-28 ([SELLAS IR](https://ir.sellaslifesciences.com/news/News-Details/2026/SELLAS-Life-Sciences-Reaches-80th-Event-in-Phase-3-REGAL-Trial-of-Galinpepimut-S-GPS-in-Acute-Myeloid-Leukemia/default.aspx)).
  - A secondary summary says topline was guided for "this quarter" ([BiopharmaWatch](https://www.biopharmawatch.com/news/SLS/sellas-life-sciences-phase-3-regal-trial-update)). The original wording was not checked.
- **Guidance history:** earlier guidance had slipped several times, from "by year-end 2025" to H1 2026 ([Barchart](https://www.barchart.com/story/news/33960176/sellas-life-sciences-announces-independent-data-monitoring-committee-periodic-review-and-positive-recommendation-to-continue-pivotal-phase-3-regal-trial-of-gps-in-aml-without-modification), [boerse-express](https://www.boerse-express.com/news/articles/sellas-life-sciences-80-event-erwartet-h1-2026-897046)).
- **Result:** from 2026-10-28, "a binary readout is due within weeks" was public. Its date and outcome were not.

## Conclusion

- A spike like this is a binary clinical event. Its direction cannot be forecast from price, volume, or MCC's indicators.
- What was knowable:
  1. A readout was pending in a known window. This is public and is a **risk** flag as much as an opportunity: the same event could have gone −70%.
  2. The move was in progress at 06:30 AZ in pre-market. Detecting it is possible; predicting it is not.
