## Deep dives — first four by scanner rank

### 1. ADXN — Addex Therapeutics

**Scanner:** Score 201.58; ON win 75%; average ON +5.26%; median +2.58%; 5D +12.83%; current ON +59.17%.

**Catalyst:** On November 11, Addex announced its spin-out Neurosterix closed a $105 million Series B. Addex did not participate; its interest is now 8.5%, valued at $18.6 million. The funding supports Neurosterix's planned Phase 2 NTX-253 schizophrenia program and broader pipeline. This is a plausible explanation for event-driven attention, but it is not equivalent to $105 million being raised by Addex itself.

**Balance-sheet/dilution caution:** Addex's September 28 half-year update reported CHF 0.767 million cash at June 30, a CHF 3.456 million H1 net loss, and subsequent ATM sales generating USD 2.8 million gross through August 25. The spin-out financing is positive validation for the asset ecosystem, but Addex's retained economic interest is minority and its own cash needs/dilution remain important.

**Assessment:** Highest raw scanner score, but I would not make it the preferred actionable candidate without verifying the +59.17% current move, price/volume continuity, float, spread, halt history, corporate action and exact catalyst timestamp. Treat as an event-risk watch, not a clean statistical setup. A 59% current Overnight move versus a 5.26% average is a major extension warning.

Sources: Addex November 11 Neurosterix financing announcement (SEC exhibit); Addex September 28 half-year results.

### 2. CECO — CECO Environmental

**Guidance:** 2026 orders outlook raised to >$2.5 billion; free-cash-flow conversion raised to approximately 100% of Adjusted EBITDA; revenue maintained at $1.30–$1.375 billion and Adjusted EBITDA at $200–$225 million. The scanner's positive 5D and current ON readings are directionally supportive, but the small median versus mean (+0.67% vs +3.21%) suggests a positively skewed return distribution; sample count is absent, so reliability cannot be judged. Verify gap levels, liquidity and post-earnings price acceptance.
